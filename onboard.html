05 / CO-DRIVER CORNER

Read the road.

A quick guide to common rally pace-note language. Exact calls and notation vary between crews.

RALLY BASICS / 01
01
PACE NOTES

A shorthand description of the road ahead, called by the co-driver so the driver can prepare for corners and hazards before seeing them.

02
CORNER GRADING

Numbers commonly describe corner severity. The scale and direction can differ between crews, so never assume one universal system.

03
DISTANCE CALLS

Measurements such as metres help the driver judge when the next corner, crest or junction is approaching.

04
CAUTIONS & HAZARDS

Calls such as “caution”, “double caution” or “jump” flag features that need extra attention or a change in approach.
